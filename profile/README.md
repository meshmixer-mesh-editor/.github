## Fast CAD Brief

What is Meshmixer? A free tool for editing and repairing triangle meshes.

What does it fix? Holes, non-manifold errors, and other mesh imperfections.  
Can it prepare prints? Yes, it adds supports and orients models for printing.  

## CAD Overview

Meshmixer occupies a distinctive niche between traditional CAD and digital sculpting, specializing in the freeform manipulation of triangle meshes rather than precise parametric solids. It is the tool people reach for when a model already exists as a mesh and needs reshaping, combining, cleaning, or preparing rather than engineering from scratch.

Its strength lies in organic operations that feel closer to sculpting clay than drafting parts. Users push, pull, smooth, and blend surfaces interactively, stamp one mesh onto another, and stitch separate objects into seamless hybrids, making it invaluable for artistic models and heavily modified scan data.

Equally important is its practical role in 3D printing. Meshmixer analyzes models for errors, fills holes, hollows solids, and generates branching support structures, turning a rough or flawed mesh into a clean, printable object ready to send directly to a slicer.
